## About

I am a biomedical engineer and neuroscience researcher preparing for doctoral study. My work and interests span experimental neuroscience, neuromodulation, neural engineering, behavioral assessment, and scientific computing.

This portfolio presents selected academic and professional work across research, data analysis, biomedical technology, digital platforms, educational content, and visual communication. It is designed to make the scope of my experience easy to review while linking visitors to supporting projects and evidence.

## Research interests

My current research focus is deep brain stimulation of the parafascicular nucleus of the thalamus in a hemi-Parkinsonian rat model. The project brings together experimental and computational approaches, including:

- Preclinical Parkinsonian models and behavioral neuroscience
- Stereotaxic procedures and deep brain stimulation
- Behavioral observation and assessment
- Neural engineering and biomedical instrumentation
- Signal processing, statistics, and scientific visualization

My broader doctoral interests include translational neuroscience, neurocognitive neuroscience, neural engineering, and neuromodulation. The website describes research direction and methods without presenting unpublished findings as established results.

## Areas of specialization

- **Neuroscience research:** Experimental research, neuromodulation, behavioral assessment, and research documentation.
- **Data science and scientific computing:** Python and MATLAB workflows, statistical analysis, signal processing, machine learning, and data visualization.
- **Digital marketing:** Search-focused content strategy, website operations, and digital campaigns for technical and healthcare-related platforms.
- **Content creation:** Research and educational writing, presentations, figures, video, and digital media for technical audiences.
